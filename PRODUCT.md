# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two audiences treated equally:
1. **Contractors / builders / project engineers** — making bulk or spec purchasing decisions, need technical specs (profile, thickness, applications), starting prices, and a fast path to a quote or technical consultation.
2. **Homeowners** — researching roofing materials for their own house, need education (what's the difference between clip-lock and snap-lock, what's PU foam for), trust signals, and a simple path to contact sales.

Both are in Thailand, browse heavily on mobile, and typically arrive via Facebook, LINE, or search while comparing suppliers before reaching out.

## Product Purpose

หจก.มีชัยสตีล (Meechai Steel) is a Thai metal roofing and insulated panel supplier — metal sheet, PU foam, bolt-type / clip-lock / snap-lock panel systems. The site exists to build enough trust and technical credibility that a visitor picks up LINE or the phone to talk to sales. It is a **lead-generation catalog + consultation funnel, not e-commerce**. Success = qualified inquiries via LINE/phone from both trade and homeowner visitors.

## Brand Personality

**Traditional & reliable.** Long-established, straightforward, family-business credibility — decades of factory experience, not a slick startup. Voice is plain, confident, and helpful rather than salesy.

## Anti-references

No specific anti-references supplied. Default guardrail: this must read as a real industrial/building-materials supplier, not a generic SaaS/tech landing page — avoid hero-metric templates, gradient text, glassmorphism, tiny tracked eyebrows, and other startup-landing-page tropes.

## Design Principles

1. **Trust before slickness** — credibility signals (factory photos, standards/certifications, testimonials, years in business) carry more weight than visual polish.
2. **Two front doors, one destination** — homeowner-facing and contractor-facing content both funnel toward LINE/phone contact, never a cart.
3. **Specs are content** — profile/thickness/application/starting-price tables are first-class page content, not buried in a PDF.
4. **Plain, bilingual clarity** — Thai-first copy with an English toggle, no unexplained jargon.
5. **Local presence, real place** — branches and the technical team are shown as concrete, findable, human — not abstract "24/7 support" claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA baseline: ≥4.5:1 text contrast, full keyboard navigation, and layouts that stay readable at mobile sizes given typically longer Thai-script line lengths.
